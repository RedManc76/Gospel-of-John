Privacy Policy – John’s Gospel App

Effective date: 17 June 2026

1. Overview

John’s Gospel App is designed to help users explore John's gospel account of Jesus life and ministry through structured sessions, video commentary, and teaching materials. The app provides access to external video and web content, primarily hosted on YouTube and other public webpages.

We respect your privacy and aim to keep this app simple, transparent, and free from unnecessary data collection.

2. Information We Collect

We do NOT collect personal data

  • We do not collect personal information such as name, email address, or phone number
  • No user accounts or sign-in are required
  • No personal data is stored on external servers
  • No tracking of identity or behaviour across apps or websites

3. Internet Access and External Content

The app requires internet access for:

  • Streaming or opening YouTube videos and playlists
  • Opening external webpages from links inside the app

These services are provided by third parties such as YouTube.

When you open external content:

  • You are subject to the privacy policies of those third-party services
  • We do not control or store any data collected by those services
  • We are not responsible for external content or practices

We recommend reviewing YouTube’s privacy policy for more information.

4. Analytics and Tracking

  • No user behaviour tracking
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No cookies or persistent tracking identifiers

5. Data Storage

The app may store minimal settings locally on your device, such as:

  • Text size preferences
  • Theme or display settings

This data is stored only on your device, is not transmitted anywhere, and can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

6. Third-Party Services

  • YouTube
  • External websites linked within the app

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

7. Children’s Privacy

This app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any data from children or adults.

Since no personal data is collected, there is no risk of user data being stored or shared.

8. Security

Since the app does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no personal information stored on our servers.

We take reasonable steps to ensure the app functions securely and reliably when accessing external content.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect app changes or legal requirements. Updates will be posted within the app or app listing.
